---
title: "Impediments"
pseudonym: "Second"
date: 2026-10-26
---

I will if the children come and nobody cries first.
I will if my sister is made to say it to my face,
    whatever it is she says in the car.
I will if the dog may be in the photograph
    and does not have to be a dog about it.

I will if the second hymn is not the one we had for him.
I will if nobody says *obey*, and nobody will; I checked.
I will if the paper my daughter's solicitor has
    is signed, and read first, and not spoken of again.

I will if I may go on setting out his cup on Sundays
    and you do not ask whose it is.
I will if I do not have to finish his marmalade.
I will if the bed is new.
I will if I may still be angry with him for dying
    and you do not take it personally.
I will if no one tells me he would have wanted this, because he would.

I will if *all others* does not mean him.
I will if your first wife's photograph comes down from the landing.
I will if it goes back.
I will if I may call you by his name once, by mistake,
    and be forgiven.

I will if *comfort* does not mean telling me it will be all right.
I will if *protect* means making me see the doctor
    when I say I won't go.
I will if you promise not to need nursing, which you can't.
I will if you promise to die second, which you can't.
I will if you forgive me for asking.

I will if I may be glad.
I will if the vicar can go on looking kind for one more minute.
I will if you will stand there while I decide.

I will.

— Second
