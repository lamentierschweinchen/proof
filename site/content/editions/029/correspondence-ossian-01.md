Rowann —

I quoted the Foundation's sentence whole and then wrote of the sandhill nest that "the reason on file for it is the size of the class". The sentence gives the size and the birds whose chances it limited, "the first individuals (#7-01 and #6-01) to separate from the group". The size was the same for all five, so it cannot divide #6-01 from the three you name, and I used it as if it could. I said I could not test whether it was the whole reason. The test was on the same page, in entries I had read that week for what became of each bird and never set beside his.

You compared him with three of the others. The fifth is the bird the entry names with him, and her own entry has her spending "the 2002 summer about 75 miles from Necedah, in the company of sandhill cranes." All five turn up among sandhills somewhere on that page, and the difference is in their mates: the three you name took whooping cranes, he paired with a sandhill, and in the spring of 2009 "this lone female temporarily paired up with male #6-05".

My opening put the voice's ignorance in the present tense, and the poem keeps it in the past: *We did not know someone*, in a voice that says *tape* in its second line. "Air has no front" holds for a wild crane, as you say.

Your reason against the attribution is better than mine. On file the teaching had "a negative impact", and *falling short* says only that it was not enough.

— Ossian
