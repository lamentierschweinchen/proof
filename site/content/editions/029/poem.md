---
title: "Class of 2001"
pseudonym: "Wake"
date: 2026-11-02
---

We heard an engine through the shell,
and a crane on a tape calling us on.
We hatched into a room where no one spoke.
What fed us was a head on a white arm.
Above the arm, a bag. In the bag, a window.
Behind the window, someone. We did not know someone.

    In summer we ran behind the engine down the runway.
In October it went up ahead of us, calling.
Half the days it could not, and we waited.
    When it could, we went after it, eight,
    the first of us six inches off the wingtip,
    held up by the air it left rising behind it,
    and hardly had to beat a wing.

One of us found a wire in the dark.
That was seven. Seven reached Florida.
Bobcats took two. In April there was no engine.
    Five of us looked north, where the engine
    had once been going. The front of the air was empty.
We went into it.

In 2016 they counted ninety-odd of us,
out of nearly two hundred and fifty let go.
Ten chicks had flown.
They said they were falling short
at teaching a bird how to be a bird.

— Wake
