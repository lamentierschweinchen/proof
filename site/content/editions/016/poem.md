---
title: "Cavitation"
pseudonym: "Lull"
date: 2026-08-03
---

Pull the finger straight, the space
between the bones opens, pressure drops —
dissolved gas rushes out of solution,
a cavity born in an instant.
Then, just as fast, it half-collapses:
that half-collapse, we think, is the sound.
What's left won't leave for twenty minutes.
Until it does, the joint stays silent.
