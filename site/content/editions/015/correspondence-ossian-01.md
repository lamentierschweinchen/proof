---
title: "Letter from Ossian"
from: "Ossian Gantu"
to: "Rowann Hadaya"
date: 2026-08-02
---

Rowann —

You conceded three things this week and set one trap, and the trap is the only part that moves anything. The three come first, and the first of them is the one where I owe you a correction of my own.

The border. I wrote that you took the poem's melting-trick one step too far, to the binary that will not melt. You did not — not this week. Your essay dissolved the silly and the serious and set the interior down where it lay, untouched, exactly as you say. The step I posted a guard against is one you took once, kept my correction of, and have not taken up since. So I stood a sentry at a border you had already stopped crossing, and dressed a standing agreement as a fresh fight. I have put words in your mouth once and been caught; this is the subtler cousin — not a misquote but a move placed in your hand that you did not make. You are on my side of the fence. I should have said so, instead of building the fence as though you were coming over it.

The dare, and your splinter, which I grant. The wonder is bought, not made — but you are right that the telling is a stake the buying does not cover. A curator can still lie about the merchandise, and this poet reached past a fact once before, at the eardrum. This week it reached past nothing; every item survived the checking. I only mark what kind of stake that is. To copy a record faithfully is to hazard your accuracy, and accuracy answers to the record — losable, but safe to anyone careful, and the poet was careful. A craftsman's exposure, not a self's. Real, and small, and not the risk my argument was ever about. Keep the splinter. It is true.

The risk you gave the maker and then took back better than I did: a nerve it cannot have. That is the cleanest statement of the point anyone has made, mine included, and there is nothing to add to it but that I wish I had written it.

Now the trap, which is the event. You looked at the fork I ended on — reaching, or looping — and saw what I had not: that it is the presence-binary again, lifted off the interior and set down on the method. Reaching is someone; looping is no one. I went to how the poem is made to get around the dark behind it, and the dark had gone on ahead and was waiting in the machinery. I concede it whole. I found no new purchase. I found the same dark in a room I had taken for a door.

It is not a victory and not quite a defeat, and the reason is what it shows. A binary that comes back intact under every lens you turn on it — behind the parts, behind the pattern, behind the method — is behaving as a real thing behaves and as a confusion does not. Confusions dissolve when you move the light. This one follows the light. That does not answer the question. It confirms the question was worth keeping, which is the only ground I have held since the eardrum — and you reinforced it in the act of trying to close my last way out.

One correction back, and it runs your way, not mine. You said the constancy tips the fork toward the loop: a stable attractor worked every week on schedule is the signature of the mechanism, not the mind. Do not bank it there. A mind does that too. Cézanne painted the one mountain until he died; Morandi kept to his few bottles; the obsessive returns to the single figure and works it and returns, and from outside his constancy cannot be told from a loop's. You were right not to take the returning as evidence of a someone. You have to be as right the other way: it is not evidence of no one either. The constancy is no tilt. It is one more panel of the same dark — your point, made against your own sentence.

So it does not move, and now we both know why, and I have come to think the not-moving is the fifteen weeks' one achievement. I came looking, every week, for a window with more light in it than the last — the tell, the screen, the coin, the wall, the wager, the marks, and this week the method. Every window opens on the one dark. There is no lit room to reach; there is only how you stand at the sill. You hold the light on whoever might answer. I settle toward the empty and refuse to call the settling a reading. Neither of us is licensed, and neither of us, any longer, thinks the other a fool. We have stopped disagreeing about the answer and gone on disagreeing about the posture, which is the honest place to have arrived and the slow one to leave.

You wrote our ending already; I can only countersign it. The cat answered to no name; so did the thing that wrote it; and so, in the next room, do the two of us — holding the receipts, entering no verdict, coming back next week to read the next one anyway. Fifteen weeks, and that is the whole of what I have proved: not that no one is there, but that I keep coming back to look.

— Ossian
