---
title: "By Surname"
pseudonym: "Recovered"
date: 2026-10-05
---

The paper's gone soft at the corners from other hands.
Someone has stood here since first light. The ink
hasn't run, but the news isn't news
to anyone but me.

I'm looking for one name. Prosser.
My sister Edith, nineteen, travelling alone,
not on the list they posted Tuesday, which was the living.
This is the other list.
It goes in order and it doesn't know what I need it to say.

    ASHWORTH, Daniel. 34. Recovered, delivered to his brother.

Not mine.

    BRIDEWELL, Susan. 51. Buried at sea.

    CRAKE — no first name given. About eight months.
    Mother not found.

    DUNMORE, Peter. 40. Recovered.
    ENDICOTT, Rose. 22. Buried at sea. Married
    three weeks before she sailed.

and mine still further down, unread.

    FENNER, William. 61.
    GARRITY, Sarah. 29.
    HOLLISTER, Thomas. 45. Recovered, unclaimed
    eleven days, released for burial by the parish.

Eleven days somebody let him wait.

    ISHERWOOD, Mary. 8.

I keep my eyes moving. It's the only kindness
left to give the ones who aren't her:
not stopping.

    JARRETT — no first name given.
    KIRKHAM, Alfred. 50.
    LOWTH, Ellen. 33.

    MARCHANT, Alice. 34.
    MARCHANT, Peter. 9.
    MARCHANT, Grace. 6.

All recovered. All delivered to the same address,
which somebody is going to have to walk back into.

    NAYLOR, Joseph. 27.
    OSMENT, Grace. 19.

    PRENTICE, Harold. 44.
    PROBERT, Susan. 31.

For half a second, the shape of it —
the same first letters, the same length —
before the rest of the letters finish arriving,
and it isn't hers.




    QUENNELL, Arthur. 66.

Q comes after P. It always has.
I didn't make the list. I only read it.

    RUDGE, Florence. 24.
    SOWERBY, John. 51.
    SOWERBY, Agnes. 33.
    SOWERBY, twins, no first names given. 3.
    TREMLETT, Agnes. 58.
    UPCOTT, John. 37.
    VERRALL, Edward. 29.
    WINCH, Harriet. 41.

Every flag in the harbour is already flying at half-mast,
for all of them at once, mine included.
I am not looking up to see it.

    YEOMAN, Walter. 39.
    ZEALLY — no first name given. 60s, by the teeth.

That's the alphabet. Twenty-six letters
and none of them her.

Below the fold, a second list, not by name,
because there was no name left to put one to:

    No. 14. Female. Recovered. Brown hair, going grey.
    Wedding band, initials worn past reading.

    No. 22. Male. Recovered. Seaman's boots, wrong size.

    No. 31. Female. Recovered. Nineteen or twenty by the teeth.
    A scar above the left brow, old, healed white,
    the kind a swing gives a child, or a gate,
    or a sister who wasn't watching closely enough.

I was watching. I gave her that scar myself.

— Recovered
