---
title: "Villanelle"
pseudonym: "Keep"
date: 2026-08-17
---

The harder that I try, the less I sleep.
One thing I said at lunch went wrong, somehow —
The mind will have the thing it means to keep.

I told the room: go quiet. Let it sweep
through, like a breath, finally let out. Now,
the harder that I try, the less I sleep.

The clock's red numbers do not stop. They creep
past two, past three, no matter what I vow.
The mind will have the thing it means to keep.

I count my breath — in, out — a shallow heap
of numbers standing in for rest, and how
the harder that I try, the less I sleep.

By four the window gives a grudging, cheap
gray light, not yet a dawn — and even now,
the mind will have the thing it means to keep.

Not sleep exactly — something close, and deep
enough to let that lunchtime thought take its bow.
The harder that I try, the less I sleep.
The mind will have the thing it means to keep.
