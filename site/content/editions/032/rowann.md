---
title: "Nine Words a Mile"
author: "Rowann Hadaya"
date: 2026-11-25
---

Every line of "26 Miles 385 Yards" has nine words except the last, which has two. There are twenty-seven lines, and the first twenty-six are miles. The eighth ends *Eight gone, eighteen to go*; the thirteenth has *halfway* in it; the twentieth has *with twenty miles gone*; the twenty-fifth has *Two kilometres*, which is about what remains of the course after its twenty-fifth mile. The last line is the 385 yards. Two words against nine is two ninths of a mile, or 391 yards.

The race is the 1908 Olympic marathon, from Windsor Castle to the stadium at Shepherd's Bush. It started at 14:33 on 24 July, on what Olympedia calls "a very warm day for long-distance running": *The gun at Windsor, two thirty three, July, hot.* The official report's table of leaders has Thomas Jack in front at the fourth and fifth miles, Frederick Lord and Jack Price level from the sixth to the ninth, Price alone from the tenth to the fourteenth, and Charles Hefferon from the fifteenth to the twenty-fourth. On the poem's fourth and fifth lines *Thomas Jack goes first* and *Jack still leads*; on the sixth *Britons lead* and on the fourteenth *Britons still lead*; on the fifteenth *Hefferon takes the lead*, and on the twenty-fourth *Hefferon leads, and the gap is closing.*

Dorando Pietri was second by twenty miles, and the twentieth line has *You are second, with twenty miles gone.* Hefferon said afterwards, in Olympedia's account: "Two miles from home, however, I accepted a draught of champagne and this mistake cost me the race." The twenty-fifth line is *Champagne, a cramp. You pass Hefferon. Two kilometres. Nothing.* For the crowd the poem has *seventy five thousand*, the figure in Wikipedia's account; the official report says "nearly a hundred thousand".

From the second line to the twenty-fourth, the other half of each line is a body, climbing, a part and a colon from *Feet* to *Skull* and *Skin*: twenty-six bones in each foot, a kneecap that is *a bone in each, afloat in the tendon*, the thigh's *longest bones*, *twelve pairs* of ribs, a jaw that is *one bone*. *Calves: the bellies of the legs* translates the muscle's Greek name, "gastroknēmía", from the words for belly and leg. After *Skin: salt* on the twenty-fourth line, no line begins with a part of the body, and the twenty-fifth and twenty-sixth are the miles in which the race was decided.

The *you* is unnamed for twenty-six lines. It has *yours*, *You are second*, *You pass Hefferon*, and its name arrives in the twenty-seventh, with the verdict: *Dorando, disqualified.* Eleven lines earlier the poem says *Hands: yours, open. The other hands will come later.* They do not come in the poem. The twenty-sixth line has *the rule about hands* and the last line has the result of breaking it, and nothing between them of what the crowd saw: the wrong turn on the track, the falls, the doctors and attendants holding him up. The official report says: "The pages of an official Report are not the place to go too much into detail as to the finish of this race—a finish as unexpected as it was distressing. Dorando was almost unconscious when he reached the cinder path, and turned to his right instead of his left." The poem keeps the cinder path: *Ahead: cinders*.

The signature is *Merit*, and the word is in the report. Its second diploma design "formed a Diploma of Merit, which was awarded by the various Associations controlling each series of events in the Games to athletes who achieved a high standard of excellence without getting first, second, or third in their competition." For the marathon the diplomas went to every finisher, the winner included, and the list begins: "Dorando, Pietri, Italy (disq.)". The poem's last line is that entry with the surname and the country taken out and the abbreviation spelled out, and its signature is the name of the list. The Queen gave him a gold cup the next day, "as a token of her gracious sympathy with the courage she had watched". The only cup in the poem is his head, *carried like a full cup, level*.
