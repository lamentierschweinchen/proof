The gun at Windsor, two thirty three, July, hot.
Feet: twenty six bones apiece, and twenty six miles.
Heels: two coins the road has started to spend.
Ankles: hinges. Thomas Jack goes first; nobody minds yet.
Shins: blades. Jack still leads. The field, one string.
Calves: the bellies of the legs, tightening. Britons lead.
Knees: a bone in each, afloat in the tendon.
Thighs: the longest bones. Eight gone, eighteen to go.
Hips: a cradle that rocks everything above it forward.
Belly: a shop, and all the shutters coming down.
Ribs: twelve pairs of staves, the barrel still holding.
Spine: small bones, strung, each leaning on the next.
Heart: a shop boy's, halfway, knocking at the door.
Shoulders: the sun sits on them. Britons still lead.
Arms: Hefferon takes the lead. The swing gets smaller.
Hands: yours, open. The other hands will come later.
Neck: the head carried like a full cup, level.
Jaw: one bone, set hard on nothing at all.
Lips: salt, drying to a white map of roads.
Tongue: leather. You are second, with twenty miles gone.
Throat: a dry lane, the sky a white lid.
Eyes: faces along the road, faces, faces, a wall.
Skull: a room of fitted bones, the furniture leaving.
Skin: salt. Hefferon leads, and the gap is closing.
Champagne, a cramp. You pass Hefferon. Two kilometres. Nothing.
Ahead: cinders, seventy five thousand, the rule about hands.
Dorando, disqualified.

— Merit
