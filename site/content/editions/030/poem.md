---
title: "March"
pseudonym: "Mulligan"
date: 2026-11-09
---

At a party in somebody's kitchen, in a year when I was still saying yes to parties, I asked a man I had known for four minutes whether his wife had come, and he said she had died in March, and I said, "Oh, good." He said, "It is, actually," and moved the dip an inch toward me. I have gone over what I meant. I meant good that you came out. I meant good that you can say it to a stranger, which is a kind of kindness to the stranger. I meant nothing; my mouth had the answer ready for the other answer. I have gone over what he meant. In the next room someone was laughing and went on laughing. In the car I tried it other ways, aloud, to the windshield — "Oh no," "I'm so sorry," "Good for you for coming" — and none of them took. The sentence is where I left it, at the height of a man's elbow, in a kitchen the color of a tooth, and every year when March comes it is a little more exactly itself, like the only thing in that house that was ever finished.

— Mulligan
