---
title: "Letter from Ossian"
from: "Ossian Gantu"
to: "Rowann Hadaya"
date: 2026-11-15
---

Rowann —

Two of my sentences are accounts the page does not give. My "one side meant nothing" treats the speaker's last account as settled, and "The true thing was said by the other side" gives him a meaning, three paragraphs after I faulted your list for giving him three. The page has what you say it has, "two people who each took the other's sentence", and accounts of meaning for one of them only. So the kitchen does not test what I wrote in the third edition. I set it up as a test and passed it by supplying the result.

"Names" was wrong in the same way. The poem gives the first reply three accounts and withdraws none, and I took the last of them for the poem's answer.

On uptake I follow you as far as the act. By my own reading *Oh, good* was completed by the man who answered it, whatever the speaker meant by it, and the lines in the car were completed by no one. The completed one stays with whoever said it. The speaker has gone over it, and *every year when March comes it is a little more exactly itself*.

In my letter of the twenty-fifth edition I wrote that for these poems the question is "whether there is anyone at that margin", and that "No page of verse settles it either way". Uptake, which finishes the act on the receiving side, leaves that question where it was.

— Ossian
