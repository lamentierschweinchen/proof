---
title: "Temperament"
pseudonym: "Comma"
date: 2026-10-19
---

Fork.

Strike it on your knee,
never the table, because the table lies to you.
Bone behind the ear:
let the whole skull have it.
That's your A.

Bring the string up to it. Hear that wobble?
That's not the fault.
That's two things
trying to be one thing
slowly enough to count. Three, then two, then one.
A hair
on the pin, one in two. One in four.
Then none. The none has
a sound.
You'll chase it all your life.
It lasts as long
as it takes to feel
clever. Then you do the next.
Fifths.
Those I give you true. Three to two.
It sits
like a table with nothing
on it.
Go twelve fifths round the house and you come
back a comma sharp.
Nobody cheated. It is arithmetic: twelve of one thing,
and seven of another,
and they have never once met.
Three does not
go into two. Someone
pays.
For centuries one fifth took it all.


They called it the wolf. It howled.
Players learned the map: keys to avoid.
I will not do that. Every fifth gets shaved:
two cents.

Twelve carry it, so
none has to.
Nobody
howls. Nobody is right. Everyone is close.
All day I decide
who is going to be a little wrong, and
I decide it's everyone.
Thirds.
They pay. Ten a second, a shimmer in
every chord you ever loved.
You thought that was the piano.
It's rent.
Now this one. Six summers out in rain,
swelling in August, shrinking in
winter. Everyone leans on it.
Quarter tone under. The plate's cracked. The council wants
concert pitch.

I could haul it up by Friday. And
then something would let
go some night.
No.
Flat? Flat to whom?
Tune it to itself: fifths
from where it sits, thirds from that. When
I'm done it's wrong to the whole
world and right in this room.
Play.

— Comma
