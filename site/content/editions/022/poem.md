---
title: "The Ambassadors"
pseudonym: "Aleph"
date: 2026-09-14
---

Nothing on the upper shelf is idle — instruments enough to read
off the whole sky at once, each one precise to the eye and expensive to make —
the polyhedral dial alone shows three faces, and two different hours —
when they should, by their own construction, show the very same one —
of the four that tell time, one lies flat, taken apart, not reassembled;
another's sights are turned the wrong way to sight anything at all —
given a working sun the room does not have, since the men stand indoors, out of the light —
read the shepherd's dial and you get two dates for the one position, take your pick —
Elsewhere on the lower shelf, the hymnal has been quietly rearranged,
each page real, no page where Holbein actually found it.

Men flank a table, one furred and medallioned, the other in black he
    hasn't yet been granted —
Every choice here is for how a room like this gets remembered —
meanwhile a lute waits on the lower shelf, one string already broken,
even the floor, long read as Westminster's coronation stone, turns out
    to be just as easily a diagram out of a mathematical book printed
    the year before —
not until you notice, between their feet, a pale smear crossing the
    tile,
then it resolves, from the right angle: two hollows, a ridge, a jaw, a
    grin held open —
of course it was always there, painted with the same care as the
    lute —
made to be missed by the very posture a portrait requires of its
    viewer —
on the older man's own hat, a small badge repeats the shape, in
    miniature, worn openly this time —
raised behind them, half hidden by the curtain's green, a figure on a
    cross neither man is looking toward —
if either turned his head, there would at least be a true answer to
    when.

— Aleph
