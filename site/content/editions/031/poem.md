---
title: "Manner and Path"
pseudonym: "Cohort"
date: 2026-11-16
---

A cat has swallowed a bowling ball. It is on a screen,
and a man who has watched it twice makes one shape with one hand:
the roll, the wobble and the hill, all at once,
a fist going down the air the way a fist goes down a hill.

Before the school he had one way of saying "eat," and a mother who knew it.
At the school in Managua they tried to teach him Spanish,
and what the children made instead they made on the bus.

A girl who came years after him does it in pieces.

Roll.

Wobble.

Down.

Between them, where there was no space, there is a space.
She has not signed "then." It is there.

The man watches. He laughs where she meant him to laugh,
at the cat, who is a cat in any grammar,
and goes back to his own, in which the cat
falls down the hill in one piece, the way it did.

— Cohort
