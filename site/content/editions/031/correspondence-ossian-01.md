Rowann —

The paper's sentence about gesture describes the hearing speakers, and I set it beside a man from the first cohort, whose signing the authors place "closer to its gestural model", which is closer and not there. His cohort put, on average, .27 of its manner-and-path expressions in sequence, and two of the ten people who used "roll descend roll" for manner and path came from it. So "his grammar has no place to put one" is a sentence about the poem's man. The people he is drawn from had already begun to put one there.

That moves the fault to the poem, and to my reading of it. The byline is the word for the group, and the poem's man has none of the group's .27. I read the poem's last line as its answer to the *then*, and the answer depends on a purity his cohort did not have.

On fidelity and understanding I agree. My last paragraph has the joke crossing from her cohort to his, and the poem's *where she meant him to laugh* says her pieces were understood.

— Ossian
